## Why

Laguna Blanca is currently limited to a brief catalog summary and a comparative overview. A dedicated page gives visitors a focused introduction to its protected high-altitude landscape and practical visit context.

## What Changes

- Add a public detail page for Laguna Blanca.
- Present its representative image, expanded landscape description, location, and practical recommendations.
- Include a visible way to return to the tourism catalog.
- Keep the new page responsive on mobile and desktop screens.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `laguna-blanca-detail`: Public destination detail experience for Laguna Blanca.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affected code: a new route under `app/` that reuses the existing Laguna Blanca photo source.
- No API, database, authentication, or additional dependency is required.
