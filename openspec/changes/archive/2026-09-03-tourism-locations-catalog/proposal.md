## Why

The project needs a useful first experience for people exploring tourism in Catamarca instead of the default application template. A small public catalog establishes the content model and visual foundation without requiring an administration system or backend.

## What Changes

- Replace the default home page with a public catalog of tourist places in Catamarca.
- Present a curated initial set of places with their name, locality, short description, category, and representative image.
- Make the catalog usable on mobile and desktop screen sizes.
- Set page metadata and primary content in Spanish for the Catamarca tourism context.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `tourism-catalog`: Public discovery and presentation of tourist places in the province of Catamarca.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affected code: `app/page.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`, and `app/globals.css`.
- No API, database, authentication, or additional dependency is required for this initial version.
