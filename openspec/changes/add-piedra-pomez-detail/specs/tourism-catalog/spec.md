## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Public tourism catalog
The system SHALL display a public catalog of tourist places in the province of Catamarca on the home page and SHALL let visitors open the Campo de Piedra Pomez detail page from its catalog entry.

#### Scenario: Visitor opens the catalog
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the home page
- **THEN** the visitor sees a collection of tourist places in Catamarca

#### Scenario: Visitor opens Campo de Piedra Pomez details
- **WHEN** a visitor selects the Campo de Piedra Pomez catalog entry
- **THEN** the system navigates to its public detail page
