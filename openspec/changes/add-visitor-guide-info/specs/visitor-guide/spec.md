## Purpose

Provide visitors with orientative trip-planning information that complements a tourist destination without presenting bookings or live commercial availability.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Nearby places information
The system SHALL present a sample of nearby places visitors can consider when planning a visit to Campo de Piedra Pómez.

#### Scenario: Visitor explores nearby places
- **WHEN** a visitor views the Campo de Piedra Pómez page
- **THEN** the visitor can identify a sample of nearby places to include in their itinerary

### Requirement: Local food and lodging information
The system SHALL present sample local food suggestions and orientative lodging options for the El Peñón area.

#### Scenario: Visitor plans services
- **WHEN** a visitor reviews the trip-planning information
- **THEN** the visitor can identify sample food and lodging options and understands that they are orientative

### Requirement: Tour availability guidance
The system SHALL state whether guided tours may be available and SHALL instruct visitors to confirm availability with local providers.

#### Scenario: Visitor checks tours
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the tours section
- **THEN** the visitor sees that tours may be available and that availability requires local confirmation
