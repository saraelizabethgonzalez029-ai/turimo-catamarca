## Why

Destination information alone does not help visitors understand how to organize a trip. An orientative visitor guide adds useful context about nearby places, local food, lodging, and tours without claiming to provide live travel services.

## What Changes

- Add a planning section to the Campo de Piedra Pómez page with sample information about nearby places to visit.
- Present local food suggestions and orientative lodging options in the El Peñón area.
- Clearly indicate that guided tours may be available and that visitors must confirm availability with local providers.
- Label all travel-planning information as orientative rather than real-time availability, pricing, or booking data.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `visitor-guide`: Orientative travel-planning information for a tourist destination, including nearby places, gastronomy, lodging, and tours.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affected code: `app/campo-de-piedra-pomez/page.tsx`.
- No API, reservation system, database, or external dependency is introduced.
